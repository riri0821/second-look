Privacy Policy

Second Look · Last updated 22 October 2026

Second Look helps you check whether a text, email or link looks like a scam. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you give it.

The short version

  • We check your message and then forget it. We do not save it.
  • If you choose to report a website or answer "Was this result right?", we save only that website name or your yes/no answer. Never your message.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account.
  • We do not show ads, sell data, or track you across other apps.
  • Your history stays on your device, and you can delete it anytime.

What we receive when you tap "Check it"

The text you pasted is sent over a secure, encrypted connection to our checking service. It is analyzed in memory and the result is sent back to you. The message is not written to a database, not saved to a file, and not included in our logs.

What we save when you report or give feedback

These buttons are optional. Nothing is saved unless you tap one.

  • Report this website as a scam: we save the website name (for example, "bdo-login.top") and a one-way code made from your connection, so the same person can't report a website twice. The code can't be turned back into your internet address, and your address itself is not saved. When several different people report the same website, Second Look warns everyone who checks it.
  • Was this result right?: we save the result shown (for example, "Likely a scam"), your yes or no answer, the language you use, and which warning checks were triggered. Not your message, not the link.

This information is stored in a database hosted by Supabase and is used only to improve Second Look's accuracy. It is not sold or shared.

Who else sees anything

To check website addresses found in your message, we may look them up with these services. Only the website address is sent, never the rest of your message.

  • rdap.org, to see when a website was created.
  • Google Safe Browsing, to see if a website is a known danger.
  • VirusTotal, to see if security companies have flagged a website.

We may also open the link from our server, without loading the page, to see where a shortened link really leads.

Technical information

Like any website, our server sees your internet (IP) address when you connect. We use it only in memory to stop abuse, by limiting how many checks one connection can make each minute, and we do not store it. Our hosting provider, Render, may keep standard connection logs (time, IP address and the page requested, not your message) for security, as described in its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your check history, lesson progress and quiz score are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. You can delete your history from the History tab. In the phone app, uninstalling the app removes everything; on the website, clearing your browser data does the same.

Children

Second Look is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not store your messages or create accounts, we hold no personal data that identifies you, so there is nothing tied to you to view, correct or delete. If you are in the Philippines, you have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and you may contact the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Changes

If we change how Second Look handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
